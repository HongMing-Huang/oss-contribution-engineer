## Cross-cutting conclusions

1. No representation wins everywhere; persistence + verification discipline (AOCI) and
   structure (LocAgent/RepoGraph) attack different failure modes. Hybrid routing is the
   defensible default (ARB: RRF hybrid beats every single family on MRR/Recall@20).
2. Budget-fit selection (Aider's binary search; ARB's BCY; AOCI's S-Quota) is the
   maturity marker of any context strategy — V3 adopts token-budget-first thinking.
3. Verification discipline separates research demos from production systems: AOCI's
   SHA-bound entries, SWE-agent's lint gate, LocAgent reading located source. V3 keeps
   "cognition selects, source proves".
4. Evaluation must be line-level and budgeted (SWE-Explore) and task-typed (ARB);
   file@top-k alone saturates.
